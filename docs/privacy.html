Privacy Policy — TeeBox

Effective date: 2026-09-01

Introduction

TeeBox ("we", "us", "our", "TeeBox") provides a social golf platform that helps golfers store, analyze, and share rounds, scorecards, and related activity data. This Privacy Policy explains what personal and activity information we collect, how we use and share it, your choices, and how to contact us. By using the TeeBox mobile app and services you accept the practices described in this policy.

What this policy covers

  • Data you provide directly (account sign-up, profile, uploads).
  • Activity data imported from connected services or uploaded files (e.g., Garmin Golf, FIT/GPX/TCX).
  • Data automatically collected from your device when you use the app (analytics, crash reports, device identifiers).
  • How we store, share, and protect your data, and how you can access or delete it.

Information we collect

1. Data you give us

  • Account and profile: name, email address, username, profile picture, optional demographic info you provide.
  • Content you create: posted comments, public round posts, photos, course reviews.
  • Files you upload: activity files and exports (FIT, GPX, TCX, CSV, JSON).
  • Support and communications: messages you send to support, survey responses.

2. Data from connected services

When you connect a third-party service (for example Garmin Golf / Garmin Connect), we may receive activity data you authorize us to access, including: activity_id, start/end time, course name and ID (if provided), hole-by-hole scores, per-hole stats (putts, fairways, GIR), GPS traces, device model, and downloadable activity files (FIT/GPX/TCX). We store only data you explicitly consent to share.

We store third-party identifiers and, where applicable, OAuth tokens to retrieve and refresh authorized data. Tokens are stored encrypted and used only to fetch data for your account.

3. Automatically collected data

  • Device & usage: device model, operating system, app version, IP address, language, timestamps, interactions with the app.
  • Diagnostics & crash logs: error reports and usage logs to help us improve the app.
  • Location: precise or approximate location only if you grant location permission; used for course discovery and mapping.

How we use your information

We use data to:

  • Provide and operate the TeeBox service and features (round storage, scorecards, maps).
  • Let you link and import activity data from third-party services (e.g., Garmin) and show source badges such as "Data from Garmin Golf."
  • Present your rounds and activity data in your profile and social feed, according to your sharing settings.
  • Improve the app (analytics, diagnostics) and detect abuse or fraud.
  • Communicate with you (support, account notices, updates).
  • Comply with legal obligations.

How we share information

We may share data with:

  • Other users: information you post publicly or share explicitly (e.g., public round posts).
  • Connected services: when you authorize a third party (for example, connecting to Garmin), TeeBox may send or retrieve data from that service as necessary to sync rounds.
  • Service providers: third-party vendors who perform services for us (hosting, analytics, email, payment processors). We require them to protect your data.
  • Legal requests: if required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets (with notice and choices where required).

We will not sell your personal information for money. If your jurisdiction requires labeling of data sales, we do not engage in those activities.

Storage & retention

We store the raw activity files you upload and the parsed/normalized round data so we can display and reprocess them.

We retain account data while your account is active and for a reasonable period after account deletion to meet legal and operational needs (e.g., fraud prevention). Example retention policy: account data retained for up to 24 months after deletion request unless deleted sooner on user request.

Security

We protect data using industry standard measures including TLS in transit and encryption at rest for sensitive tokens. Access to production data is limited and audited.

While we take reasonable steps to protect data, no system is 100% secure — if a breach occurs we will follow applicable notification laws.

Your choices and rights

  • Account access and portability: You can access and export your personal and activity data via the app or by contacting us. Exports may include your profile, rounds, and uploaded files.
  • Deletion: You may delete your account or request deletion of imported rounds. We will honor deletion requests and remove data in accordance with our retention policy and legal obligations.
  • Disconnecting connected services: You can disconnect third‑party integrations (e.g., Garmin) in account settings. Disconnecting prevents future syncs; previously imported rounds remain unless you request deletion.
  • Marketing preferences: You can opt out of marketing emails via the unsubscribe link in emails.
  • Privacy rights: If you are located in the EU/UK/California or other jurisdictions with privacy laws, you may have additional rights (access, correction, deletion, restriction, data portability, objection, and to not be subject to automated decisions). Submit requests as described below.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 (or minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If notified that we have data from a child, we will delete it.
